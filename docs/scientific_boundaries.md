# Scientific boundaries

ItchEvi qualifies prespecified evidence claims. It does not discover causal
mechanisms, optimize candidate genes, or replace donor-level statistical
analysis.

- Donors or donor pairs are biological statistical units.
- Cells and spots are measurement units.
- Missing, failed, and not-applicable evidence are not numeric zero.
- Descriptive external direction is not formal replication.
- Spatial model weights are not measured cell proportions.
- Spatial co-localization is not a validated niche mechanism.
- Atopic dermatitis lesion evidence is not automatically itch-specific.
- Qualification does not establish causality, treatment efficacy, or a
  therapeutic target.

These boundaries apply to software documentation, manuscripts, figures, and
downstream reuse.
